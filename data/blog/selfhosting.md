I've long considered online privacy to be an important part of my personal life. I avoid social media, I'm careful with the digital footprint I leave and am selective over what software I use. One thing that's always been at the back of my mind is self-hosting. I use iCloud for most things but I also have files stored on mega, onedrive and a few other places.
